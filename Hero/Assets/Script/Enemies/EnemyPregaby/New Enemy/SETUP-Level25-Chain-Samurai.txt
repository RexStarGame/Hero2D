LEVEL 25 ORC / CHAIN ENEMY SETUP

AUTOMATIC SETUP (RECOMMENDED)

1. Let Unity finish importing the project and the split Orc PNG files.
2. In Unity's top menu choose:
   Hero2D > Enemies > Setup Level 25 Orc
3. The tool configures the existing "Chain Enemy" prefab. It does not add an
   enemy to the open scene.
4. Add "Chain Enemy" to EnemySpawn's Enemy Pool with Min Level 25.

WHAT THE TOOL CONFIGURES

- Builds normal standalone .anim files and Level25 Orc.controller from the
  100x100 Orc PNG strips:
  Idle (6), Walk (8), Attack01 (6), Attack02 (6), Hurt (4), Death (4).
- Assigns that Animator Controller to Chain Enemy.prefab.
- Uses horizontal sprite flipping because this art is side-facing, not a
  four-direction sprite set.
- Sets Rigidbody2D Gravity Scale to 0, interpolation on and rotation frozen.
- Replaces the weapon-shaped PolygonCollider2D with one stable body-only
  CapsuleCollider2D. The weapon drawn in the animation is visual only.
- Connects Attack01 to the aimed slash and Attack02 to the larger circular AOE.
- Keeps the enemy alive briefly after death so the Death animation can finish.
- Ensures EnemyCoins and HitFeedback are present.

IMPORTANT

Do not also add MageRed or EnemyAttack. ChainSamuraiLevel25 owns movement and
both attacks. Adding another movement/attack controller would make the scripts
fight each other.

COMBAT

- Attack 1: short aimed chain slash. Yellow warning cone.
- Attack 2: larger circular chain AOE. Red warning circle.
- Damage occurs after the warning/windup and is scaled by the selected game
  difficulty through EnemyDifficultyProfile.
- SafeZone protection and EnemyAggro2D authority remain shared with the other
  enemies and future co-op preparation.

TUNING

Select Chain Enemy.prefab and edit ChainSamuraiLevel25 in the Inspector:
- detection/give-up range
- movement speed
- slash/AOE range and damage
- cooldown and AOE chance
- warning colours and widths

EnemyHealth controls base health and XP. EnemyCoins controls the random minimum
and maximum coin reward.

WHY THE RAW ASEPRITE FILE IS OUTSIDE ASSETS

The original Orc.aseprite source is preserved in Hero/SourceArt, outside Unity's
Assets folder. Some Unity/Aseprite importer combinations cannot read its format.
Keeping it outside Assets prevents import errors and stops Unity from rebuilding
and erasing Animator transitions. The game uses ordinary PNG sprites, .anim
clips and a standalone Animator Controller instead.
